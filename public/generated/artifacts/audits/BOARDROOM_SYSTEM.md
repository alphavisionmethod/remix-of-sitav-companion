# SITA OS — Boardroom System

## Core Truth

The Boardroom is where advisor voices become one governed plan.
It is NOT a dashboard. It is NOT a slide deck clone.
Visible disagreement is a strength. Confidence and uncertainty must be clear.

## Boardroom Types

| Type | Cadence | Focus |
|------|---------|-------|
| Business Boardroom | Weekly | Pipeline, revenue, client health, opportunities |
| Finance Boardroom | Weekly | Cash flow, reserves, obligations, waste |
| Family Boardroom | Weekly | Household health, calendar, logistics, events |
| Opportunity Review | On-demand | Cross-domain opportunity assessment |
| Executive Review | Monthly | Full-spectrum strategic review |

## Required Sections

1. Executive Summary — 3-5 bullet synthesis
2. Priorities — Ranked action items with confidence
3. Opportunities — Revenue, savings, efficiency gains
4. Risks — Flagged concerns with severity
5. Unresolved Items — Carryover from previous sessions
6. Approvals Needed — Pending decisions
7. Proof Summary — What was verified since last review
8. Advisor Tracks — Individual specialist perspectives
9. Disagreement Cards — Where advisors conflict
10. Confidence Ladder — Visual certainty indicator
11. Next Action Queue — What happens after this session
12. What Changed — Delta since last boardroom
13. Decision Memory — Historical decisions and outcomes

## Widgets

- **Advisor Voice Track** — Individual specialist input with confidence
- **Disagreement Card** — Conflicting positions side by side
- **Confidence Ladder** — Visual 0-100 scale with color bands
- **Proof Summary Board** — Verified facts, receipts, audit trail
- **Decision Memory Strip** — Past decisions and their outcomes
- **Carryover Panel** — Unresolved items carried forward
- **Next Action Queue** — Prioritized next steps with owners

## Display Modes

- **Standard** — Full boardroom with all sections
- **Calm** — Summary + priorities only
- **Analyst** — Maximum density, all metrics
- **Presentation** — Fullscreen slides, auto-advance option
- **Executive** — Strategic focus, hide operational detail

## Carryover Logic

Items marked unresolved at end of session:
- Automatically carry to next boardroom
- Age counter increments
- Escalation after 3 carries without resolution

## Decision Memory

Every boardroom decision:
- Recorded with timestamp
- Linked to advisor recommendations
- Linked to resulting actions
- Outcome tracked over time
- Available for replay
