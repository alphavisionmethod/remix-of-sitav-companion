## PART 5 — Validation hardening

New validator: `check-service-vocabulary-coherence` (BLOCKING). Enforces:

1. **Service-as-transport** — services importing `@supabase/supabase-js` at
   runtime. *(Type-only imports excluded — they are erased by the TS compiler.)*
2. **Client-as-orchestrator** — `UI_CLIENT` files importing executor /
   approval / receipt / workflow services.
3. **Adapter-as-facade** — `src/modules/**`, `src/pages/**`, `src/admin/**`
   importing `INTEGRATION_ADAPTER` runtime entry points directly.
   *(Adapter `/types` and `/contracts` subpaths are explicitly allowed —
   they are pure contract surfaces under the Wave 1.2 model.)*
4. **Raw-SDK bypass** — code outside `src/integrations/`, `src/lib/data-services/`,
   `supabase/functions/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, and `src/__tests__/` importing
   `@supabase/supabase-js` at runtime.
